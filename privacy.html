Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What this site does

wayne-matthews.com is the official website of Wayne Matthews. This page explains what information is collected when you visit, and why.

Mailing list

If you sign up for the mailing list, your email address is collected and stored by Sender (sender.net), the email service used to send updates about new music and shows. Your email is only used for that purpose and is never sold or shared with third parties. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link included in every email.

Cookies

This site asks for your permission before setting any non-essential cookies. You can choose which categories to allow:

  • Essential — required to remember your cookie preference. Always active.
  • Functional — set by Sender (sender.net) to manage the mailing list sign-up widget.
  • Analytics — set by Google Analytics to understand site traffic. IP addresses are anonymized.

This site does not use cookies for advertising.

You can change your choice at any time via the Cookie Settings link in the site footer.

Third-party services

This site connects to a number of third-party services to provide its content and features:

  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) — embedded videos. May set cookies once a video is loaded or played, independent of this site's cookie preferences.
  • Spotify — embedded audio players. May set cookies once loaded or played, independent of this site's cookie preferences.
  • Bandsintown — powers the tour dates listing. Fetched automatically; does not set cookies on this site.
  • Fourthwall — powers the merch store. Product information is fetched automatically and does not set cookies on this site. Clicking a product takes you to merch.wayne-matthews.com, hosted by Fourthwall, which handles payment processing, order fulfillment, and customer data for purchases. See Fourthwall's privacy policy for details.
  • Google Fonts — loads this site's typefaces from an external CDN, which may log basic technical information such as your IP address as part of standard web delivery.

Refer to Google's and Spotify's own privacy policies for further details on those services.

Your rights

If you are located in the EU/EEA, you have the right under GDPR to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To make a request, contact us at info@wayne-matthews.com.
